Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

FunTests is a collection of browser-based personality quizzes. All quiz calculations happen entirely in your browser. We do not collect, store, or transmit your quiz answers or personal information.

1. Data Processing

All quiz logic — scoring, result generation, love calculation — runs locally in your browser. Your answers and names are never sent to any server.

2. Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage only for anonymous visit counting. This data stays on your device.

3. Third-Party Services

  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website. May log standard request data for security.
  • Counter API — anonymous page view counting. No personal data sent.

4. Advertising

This site may display ads via Google AdSense. Google uses cookies to serve ads. Manage preferences at Google Ad Settings or opt out at aboutads.info.

5. Children's Privacy

These quizzes are suitable for all ages. We do not collect any personal information from anyone.

6. Entertainment Disclaimer

All tests are for entertainment only and are not psychological assessments. We do not store or analyze your quiz results.

7. Contact

Questions? Visit our contact page.
