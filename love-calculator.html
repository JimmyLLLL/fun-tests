Love Calculator

The Love Calculator is a fun tool that calculates a compatibility percentage between two people based on their names. Simply enter your name and your partner's name, click calculate, and get your love score instantly!

How It Works

The calculator uses a deterministic algorithm based on the letters in both names. The same pair of names will always produce the same result, so you can share it with friends and they'll get the same score. This is purely for entertainment — no scientific basis!

Love Score Meanings

  • 90-100% — Perfect match! Soulmates 💞
  • 70-89% — Great compatibility, strong connection 💗
  • 50-69% — Good potential, work on it 💛
  • 30-49% — It's complicated, but anything is possible 💔
  • 0-29% — Better as friends? 😅

Fun Ways to Use It

  • Test with your crush and see if the stars align!
  • Compare your name with different celebrity crushes
  • Challenge friends to find the highest-scoring couple
  • Share results on social media for laughs
